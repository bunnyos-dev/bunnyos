# Hiring an artist: logo and wallpapers

**Not yet.** The maintainer decided to hire once Bunny Linux is a working OS
(it installs, starts into a desktop, and is usable day to day). Whoever
reaches that milestone first, a daily session or a live one, reminds the
maintainer and points here.

## Terms

- **Logo: bought outright.** The copyright transfers to the Bunny Linux
  project, so the project controls its name and mark. The artist may show it
  in their portfolio.
- **Wallpapers: the artist keeps the copyright** and releases them under
  **CC BY-SA 4.0**, so they can ship in every image, be changed, and be
  forked with the distribution (as Fedora and KDE do). Buying them outright
  is fine too if the artist prefers.
- **Source files** for both (vector and layered originals).
- **No AI-generated work**, so ownership is clear.
- **Paid by milestone:** concepts, chosen direction, final files, through the
  platform's escrow (Freelancehunt or Upwork).
- Ask for the logo and wallpaper prices separately.

## Credit

Written into the agreement:

- name and portfolio link in `CREDITS.md`, the Guide's About page, and the
  installer's About or final screen;
- the author in each wallpaper's metadata (shown by desktops that read it);
- a mention in the release notes of the version that first ships the work.

## Where to look

Find the artist by portfolio (Behance, Dribbble, ArtStation), then hire
through a platform with escrow: Freelancehunt (Ukraine's largest, open to
clients anywhere, English, low commission, design contests) or Upwork. The
artist doesn't have to be from any particular country.

## The post

> **Logo and wallpapers for an open-source Linux distribution**
>
> Bunny Linux is a new open-source Linux distribution focused on being calm,
> simple and pleasant to use. I'm looking for one artist to create:
>
> **1. Logo** (bunny theme): SVG, full color and one-color, versions for
> light and dark backgrounds, and a simplified mark that still reads at
> 16–32 px. It must work as a simple silhouette, because it will also appear
> as text-art in the terminal and on the boot screen.
>
> **2. Wallpapers** (3–5): original art in 4K 16:9, plus 16:10 and
> ultrawide if possible. At least one calm, low-detail version that menus
> and text can sit on top of (boot menu, installer).
>
> **Rights:** the logo's copyright transfers to the project (you can show it
> in your portfolio). Wallpapers stay yours, released under CC BY-SA 4.0 so
> they can ship with the distribution. Source files for both. No
> AI-generated work.
>
> **Credit:** your name and link in the project's credits, the
> documentation, the installer's About screen and the release notes, and in
> each wallpaper's metadata.
>
> Please share 2–3 pieces of your work closest to this, your price for the
> logo and for the wallpapers separately, and a rough timeline.

Before posting, add a short appendix from DESIGN.md's "Look and feel" (the
palette and mood), and the maintainer's own logo idea: two rabbits doing a
binky jump, chasing each other in a loop, abstract or line-based in the style
of Linux distribution logos.

## Where the art is used

- **Logo:** fastfetch and `/etc/os-release` (`sys-apps/bunny-release`, as
  text-art made from the SVG), the boot menu theme, the Plymouth start-up
  animation, the installer, the Guide and the website, icons.
- **Wallpapers:** the desktop; the calm one behind the boot menu, the
  installer and the login screen.
